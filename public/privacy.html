Privacy Policy

This website is a locally managed garage sale listing platform. Your privacy is important, and this page explains how your data is handled.

1. Information We Collect

When you use this website, we may collect the following information:

  • Address and listing details you submit
  • Photos you upload

This information is used solely to operate the listing service.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To display your garage sale listing on the map
  • To allow you to manage your own listings
  • To allow administrators to manage the platform

We do NOT sell, share, or distribute your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on this website’s server and is only used for functionality of the application.

5. Data Retention

Your submitted listings remain available until:

  • You delete them yourself
  • An administrator removes them

6. Data Deletion Instructions

If you would like your data removed from this site, you may:

  • Delete your listings directly from the "My Listings" page
  • Contact the website administrator to request complete data removal
